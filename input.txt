Chapter 1 Thriller Game, Professional Challenge


[You have been selected by the horror game to become one of the challengers in this round.]

[This round's challenge profession is taxi driver, with an overall danger level of B.]

[Every night, in this beautiful and terrifying world, many taxi drivers work diligently, providing great convenience to many eerie beings who don't have a hearse or don't want to drive one. They are an indispensable part of this horror world.]

[Taxi drivers work hard, but their income is relatively high. However, they occasionally encounter unreasonable customers, leading to some unpleasant situations.]

[You are the first batch of newly hired drivers. Tonight you will be making your first trip. What kind of strange events you will encounter, which route you will take, and where your destination will be all up to luck.]

[Please try your best to survive!]

[This professional challenge lasts for seven days. Challengers who are still alive after seven days will receive rewards commensurate with their performance.]

[The challenge officially begins.]

The cold, emotionless notification sounded, and Lin Bei's vision returned to normal; it was no longer pitch black.

His face was pale.

"Damn it, I got chosen..."

"And a taxi driver, a B-level danger level one."

"Seven days."

"Surviving will be incredibly difficult; taxi drivers will come into contact with many supernatural beings."

This horror game, which suddenly appeared years ago, pulls a group of people into a horror world every day, starting a game called a career challenge.

Once you become a challenger, you receive various job titles and must complete the corresponding tasks. Any non-cooperation will result in immediate elimination.

Completing the challenge alive will earn you corresponding rewards; the better your performance, the better the reward.

Each round of career challenges lasts varying, from three days to thirty days. The longest known challenge is to serve as the butler for a certain extremely powerful supernatural being for three years.

Death could happen at any moment during the challenge.

Failure to complete the job could lead to death, displeasing a customer could lead to death, even walking down the street could result in being killed by a ghost—it was extremely dangerous.

"Sigh~"

Lin Bei sighed and turned to look around.

He was currently in a parking lot, filled with hearses of the same model and color.

Besides him, there were eleven other people, all challengers participating in this round of the professional challenge.

Nine of them looked uneasy, fearfully looking around, constantly complaining.

"This is the worst luck I've ever had, running into this mess."

"Taxi drivers, you must encounter a lot of ghosts while working, one wrong move and you'll die..."

"Quit, I want to quit!"

"..."

Clearly, they, like Lin Bei, were newly selected challengers.

The remaining two were relatively calm, already observing the situation, clearly having relevant experience.

...

Three minutes later, a figure approached from not far away.

It was hideous, bloated, covered in swollen, dark red pustules, and had three eyes—yellow-green-red in color—always with one eye open and the other two closed.

"Hello everyone, I'm the vehicle supervisor."

"Are you the new employees who joined today?"

"Come and get your car keys."

"Choose your own. There are many cars, some in good condition, some in bad. Don't be smug if you pick a good one, and don't complain if you pick a bad one."

"Let me tell you in advance, each person's daily turnover must not be less than three hundred Terror Coins. If it's less than that, we'll make up for it with your blood and flesh or your lives."

"One by one, starting with you."

The vehicle manager randomly pointed to someone, gesturing for that person to come and get the keys.

At the same time, he pulled out a dozen or so keys from his pocket and casually threw them on the ground in front of him.

Judging from their appearance, these keys were all old; some were patina-covered, and others were rusty.

Although the person was extremely resistant, he dared not disobey orders. With a look of fear, he walked forward and randomly picked up a car key from the ground.

He pressed the unlock button, and the headlights of a taxi in the distance lit up.

A badly damaged taxi...

No windshield wipers, a third of the windshield shattered, only one rearview mirror, the left front tire completely worn out, and the car covered in scratches and dents. In the real world, this car wouldn't be allowed on the road without extensive repairs.

“Huh?”

“I have to earn 300 Terror Coins driving such a beat-up car?”

“How could that be possible!”

“What kind of monster would ride in such a dilapidated taxi…”

He was desperate.

The others also had mixed feelings.

They were all worried that they would end up driving such a broken-down car.

“Next.”

“You come.”

Another person was forced to choose a car.

Lin Bei stood at the back, frowning as he looked at the cars.

There were hundreds of cars, but two-thirds of them were broken-down.

And there were only a dozen or so keys to choose from. He already had an answer in his mind.

"Most of these keys correspond to severely damaged vehicles, just in varying degrees of damage. Even if you're lucky, you'll end up with a broken car. Reaching a turnover of 300 is really difficult."

"Headache!"

He was momentarily at a loss for what to do.

He'd already encountered a problem right from the start.

Suddenly, a cold, mechanical voice rang in Lin Bei's mind:

[Ding!]

[God-Level Favorability System Officially Activated!]

[The main function of this system is to increase the favorability points of the target. The increase rate is one point per second. It can only be used on one monster at a time. There is no upper limit to favorability, and it can be stopped at any time.]

[Once favorability is increased, it will not decrease.]

[System Level: lv1..]

[Affected Area: Wandering Soul level and below (Necromancer - Wandering Soul - Vengeful Spirit - Evil Spirit - Monster King - Monster Emperor - Disaster Level - Nightmare Level - Hell Level - Monster God.)]

[Experience Points: 0/1000 (One favorability point corresponds to one experience point)]

[Other functions will be unlocked when the system reaches a certain level!]

[The Lucky Roulette function will be unlocked after reaching lv2!]

"A system?!"

"I actually have a system!"

"And a system that can directly increase favorability points!"

"Incredible!"

Lin Bei was overjoyed.

He knew very well what a system was, having read many novels.

Suppressing his excitement, Lin Bei looked at the vehicle manager.

An information panel appeared before him.

[Traffic Light]

[Favorability Points: -10]

[Relationship: Treats you as a plaything]

[Level: Wandering Soul]

[...]

Originally a 300-pound fat man, he was hit by a taxi while running a red light one day, unfortunately crashing into the traffic light and dying.

Car destroyed, man dead, traffic light collapsed, driver fled.

After death, he merged with the traffic light, becoming this bizarre creature.

The scene he saw before he died was a taxi, so he came here to become a traffic controller, in order to find the taxi driver who hit him.

A rule-based bizarre creature!

When in its presence, you must obey traffic rules; once you run a red light, you will become its prey.

】

"-10 favorability points?"

"This guy is definitely up to no good!"

Without a second's hesitation, Lin Bei decisively decided to increase the bizarre creature's favorability.

The level just met the requirements.

"Increase favorability."

"Target: Traffic light."

【Ding!】

【Target locked!】

【Favorability has begun to increase!】



Chapter 2 Offering cigarettes and tea, brother, have a cigarette?


+1..

+1..

+1..

Only Lin Bei could see that a +1 appeared above the vehicle manager's head every second.

Ten seconds later, he checked the information panel again.

【Traffic Light】

【Affection Points: 0】

【Relationship: Just a passerby】

【..】

“It really is one point per second.”

“It’s already down to 0.”

..

The vehicle manager was completely unaware of the anomaly.

It was urging the second challenger.

“Hurry up and choose.”

“No need to hesitate.”

“The cars are all in good condition.”

After some deliberation, the second person finally chose a key based on intuition.

But the result wasn’t good; it was another severely damaged car, this time with only one door.

He stood dumbfounded in front of the car, his expression as if he’d eaten shit.

“What a piece of junk.”

“It’s worse than a toy car.”

A short while later, the third person also finished choosing.

Similarly, one broken-down car.

Then came the fourth person.

Broken-down car.

The fifth person.

Broken-down car.

The sixth person, the seventh person...

All broken-down cars!

The eighth person was one of the two non-newcomer challengers.

Seeing it was his turn, Li Hao walked forward. Just before reaching the vehicle manager, he took out a box of expensive cigarettes he had prepared beforehand.

“Manager, these are for you.”

“Necromancer's World brand cigarettes.”

Seeing this, the vehicle manager raised an eyebrow, seemingly satisfied with Li Hao's performance.

He reached out and took the cigarettes.

He tore open the packaging and pulled one out.

Li Hao, with keen insight, took out a lighter and lit the cigarette.

The vehicle manager took a satisfying drag, his mood visibly improving.

“Not bad, tastes great, I like it.”

Li Hao said with a smile:

“Then what do you think…”

He didn't finish his sentence, but the vehicle manager already knew what he meant.

The vehicle manager pulled another car key from his pocket and casually tossed it to Li Hao.

“Here.”

“This is a newly repaired car.”

“Enough for seven days.”

“Do a good job.”

Li Hao took the key and quickly pressed the unlock button. A taxi in the distance turned on its lights.

The exterior was intact, with no obvious damage, and very clean.

“Not bad.”

“Wasn’t a waste of my cigarette.”

“With this car, a little better service, and avoiding conflicts with customers, this challenge will be a breeze.”

He pressed the ignition button.

Vroom!

The engine roared to life, the dashboard lights up, and all he had to do was shift gears and accelerate.

The other challengers were stunned by this maneuver!

"Huh? You can do that? Trade a pack of cigarettes for a nice car?"

"Damn it, he's already so far ahead. If I were a monster, I'd definitely choose his car over mine, this one without even windows."

"Why didn't I think of that? If I'd known, I wouldn't have chosen this piece of junk!"

"What's the use of knowing? Do you have any Terror Coins to buy it?"

"Envious!"

"..."

Lin Bei was no exception.

He hadn't expected this kind of thing to exist; it was a closely guarded secret.

Only another challenger, not a newbie, remained calm.

He proactively said to the vehicle manager:

"Manager, can I choose a car first?"

"I've also prepared a small gift for you."

Hearing this, the vehicle manager became very interested.

"Okay."

"You choose first."

Yue Xiaoliang immediately walked towards the vehicle manager, taking out the gift he had prepared beforehand.

A large box of beautifully packaged tea.

"This is Styx Blood Tea, prepared for you."

"It has a rich aroma and can also refresh you; it's perfect for you."

The vehicle manager was very satisfied with this gift.

The tea was very practical.

He would drink it often.

"Here's a key for you."

"A car with only minor problems."

"That'll be enough for you."

Yue Xiaoliang took the key and left, receiving a perfectly good taxi in return.

Someone was unhappy.

"It's unfair! Why do they get to drive nice cars while we get to drive beat-up ones? This is clearly harassment!"

"I want a nice car too! You can't treat me like this just because I didn't give gifts!"

"It has to be fair! Everyone has to be treated equally, or we'll report you!"

"..."

They were protesting.

They all wanted a better car.

But it was useless; it only angered the vehicle manager.

"Shut up, all of you."

"One more argument and you'll be fined three thousand and kept here to work until you die!"

Instantly, everyone fell silent.

No one wanted to bear the consequences.

The challenge lasted seven days, requiring three hundred Terror Coins per day, totaling less than three thousand.

One of the strict requirements for completing the challenge was that the earnings couldn't be negative; even a single-digit amount would be considered completion. But if the earnings were negative, there would be serious consequences.

Yue Xiaoliang walked past the crowd, a disdainful look on his face.

"You think you can get preferential treatment without paying anything?"

"Dream on!"

"This is a horror game!"

"A bunch of idiots, just wait to die."

Yue Xiaoliang was hoping these people would pick a lousy car.

The more people did this, the fewer competitors there would be, the more Terror Coins he could earn, and the less danger he would face.

The other challengers didn't bother to curse Yue Xiaoliang.

They were in a panic...

Soon, the car selection process would end, and then the challenge would officially begin.

...

The vehicle manager, a cigarette dangling from his lips, placed the premium tea aside and looked at the remaining three.

"Next."

His gaze inadvertently swept over Lin Bei, then settled on him.

"Strange."

"Why does this guy look so pleasing to the eye?"

"I have an urge to sit down and have a drink with him, and have a good chat about the strange creatures."

"I don't understand."

"Let him choose."

Lin Bei was also looking at the vehicle manager.

However, he was looking at the top of his head...

+1...

+1...

The numbers kept appearing.

[Traffic Light]

[Affection Points: 55]

[Relationship: Want to Drink with You]

[...]

"Increased affection by 65 points."

"Soon to reach one hundred."

"Want to drink with me?"

"With affection at this level, he shouldn't deliberately make things difficult for me."

"But to be on the safe side, it's better to reach one hundred."

...

At this moment, the vehicle manager waved to Lin Bei.

"Buddy, why don't you choose first?"

A smile appeared on its ferocious face, making it look even more terrifying.

At this moment, everyone's attention was focused on Lin Bei.

Some were secretly cursing Lin Bei for choosing the worst car.

But no one expected Lin Bei to shake his head:

"I'll choose last."

"Let the others go first."

Everyone's expression changed; they thought Lin Bei was courting death.

The vehicle manager had just been enraged!

But now Lin Bei had done this, his attitude and tone completely impolite, even taking the lead.

"This guy's asking for it!"

"He dared to disobey that weird order; he's practically asking for death."

"What an idiot. He didn't offer cigarettes or tea; how could he expect preferential treatment?"

Actually, Lin Bei was also testing the waters.

He wanted to see how the vehicle manager would react to this request.

Another ten seconds passed, and his favorability points rose to 70.

A whole new stage.

[Traffic Light]

[Affection Points: 70]

[Relationship: Friend]

[It treats you as a friend and will help you as much as possible as long as it doesn't cross its bottom line.]

Just then, the vehicle manager reacted.

"Okay."

"Then you choose last."

"By the way, want a cigarette?"

"You've never tried a cigarette from Thriller World, have you?"

As it spoke, it walked towards Lin Bei, holding a cigarette in its hand.

Lin Bei naturally reached out and took the cigarette.

He put the cigarette in his mouth and was about to ask for a light.

But before he could even speak, the vehicle manager proactively took out a lighter and smiled as it lit the cigarette for Lin Bei.

"Have this for now."

"Just tell me if you need more."

"I'll go get busy."

With that, the vehicle manager turned and left.

Lin Bei took a deep drag of the cigarette.

A metallic taste of blood filled his mouth.

The smoke in the horror world is different from that in the ordinary world; it's not addictive, contains no harmful substances, and is more like a supplement, relieving stress while enhancing one's inner supernatural power.

"It tastes good."



Chapter 3: Affection surpasses 100, giving my brother the best car.


Everyone was stunned.

The situation hadn't unfolded as expected.

The vehicle manager not only didn't reprimand Lin Bei, but instead lit a cigarette for him, acting as if they were old friends. The two, one human and one ghost, stood together seamlessly.

"What's going on? It gave me a cigarette?!"

"I'm dumbfounded. Giving me a cigarette is one thing, but lighting it for me? Are you two having an affair?"

"Really? This ghost is so good-natured?!"

"It's like it's turned into a different ghost..."

"..."

The situation was very strange.

The vehicle manager's behavior was very odd; "outrageous" wouldn't be an exaggeration.

At this moment, someone mustered up the courage to ask:

"Manager..."

"I also want a cigarette, can I have one?"

Hearing this, the vehicle manager glared at the person, frowning impatiently.

It casually took out a key and smashed it forcefully on the person's head.

"You want a cigarette?"

"Are you worthy?"

"What right do you have to compare yourself to my brother?"

"Your task today is five hundred!"

"If you don't earn enough, I'll chop off two of your fingers!"

"You damned bastard!"

"Deadly World" brand cigarettes are expensive, usually costing three figures.

How could he just share such cigarettes with someone?

Especially this clown.

His status is lower than his, and he's under his control, yet he dares to ask for a cigarette?

What an idiot!

At this moment, everyone felt even more strange.

The contrast between the two, the difference in their treatment, was like heaven and earth.

The man was filled with regret.

"I'm such an idiot."

"Why did you ask such a question?"

"Now you've ruined yourself!"

...

Li Hao sat in the car, staring intently at Lin Bei, his hands gripping the steering wheel tightly, his eyes filled with malice.

He really wanted to get out of the car and snatch the cigarette from Lin Bei's mouth.

“Those are cigarettes I bought, a gift for that weirdo. I wouldn't even smoke them myself!”

“What right do you have to smoke cigarettes I bought!”

“This pack costs 180 Terror Coins, and there are only 20 cigarettes in it. That works out to 9 Terror Coins per cigarette.”

“Damn it!”

At the same time, Li Hao was also puzzled.

He didn't understand why the vehicle manager would offer Lin Bei cigarettes.

He was certain that Lin Bei was a newbie.

Because after becoming a formal challenger, one could view other people's information.

Information related to Lin Bei had appeared in Li Hao's view.

【Lin Bei】

【Level: Newbie】

【Completed Class: None】

【Overall Rating: None】

“A newbie, yet enjoying this kind of treatment?”

“He has a secret!”

“Could he be being kept by some powerful weirdo?”

“His looks certainly meet that requirement.”

Weirdos are similar to humans, possessing different personalities and hobbies.

Some monsters love to eat and spend their time researching food.

Some monsters love to kill and will try every means to increase their strength.

Naturally, there are also monsters that enjoy manipulating humans. Some monsters like to keep humans as pets, playing a game called a "human nurturing project," investing resources to cultivate a human so they can do other, more important things.

This is the only possibility Li Hao can think of right now.

"It should be him."

"Don't provoke him yet."

"We'll see how things develop later."

...
